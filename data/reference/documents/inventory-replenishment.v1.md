# Inventory Replenishment Procedure

## Reorder points

Each product has a reorder point equal to expected sales over the supplier lead time plus two weeks of safety stock. Reorder points are reviewed monthly.

## Supplier lead times

- Lighting, Kitchen, Decor: 3 weeks
- Textiles: 4 weeks
- Furniture: 8 weeks

## Cycle counts

Each store counts one category per week, so every category is counted at least monthly. Differences above 2% of the expected quantity are investigated before stock is adjusted.

## Discontinued products

When a product is discontinued, open purchase orders are cancelled and remaining stock is sold through at list price.
