# Store Operations Handbook

## Opening hours

Physical stores open 09:00–20:00 Monday to Saturday and 11:00–17:00 on Sunday, unless local regulations require otherwise.

## Processing a return in store

1. Check proof of purchase and that the purchase date is within the return window in the current Returns and Refund Policy.
2. Inspect the product. Damaged-in-transit claims need a photo.
3. Record the return against the original invoice and line, with a reason.
4. Refund to the original payment method.

Returns outside the window need store manager approval, recorded as a goodwill exception.

## End-of-day cash-up

The closing manager reconciles till totals with the point-of-sale report and records any discrepancy above €20 in the incident log.

## Escalation

Customer complaints that cannot be resolved in store go to Customer Operations within one business day.
