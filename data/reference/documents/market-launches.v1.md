# Market Launches and Range Changes

## France web shop launch

The French web shop, **FR Online**, launched on **2 March 2026**. Brindlewood Home had no sales in France before this date, so French sales for January and February 2026 are zero by design, not missing data. There are no physical stores in France.

## Store network

Brindlewood Home trades through physical stores in London, Dublin, Berlin, Amsterdam and Madrid, and web shops for the United Kingdom, Germany and France.

## Range changes

The **Glass Vase** (SKU DEC-5001) was discontinued on 28 February 2026 after the supplier ended production. It has no sales from March 2026 onward.
