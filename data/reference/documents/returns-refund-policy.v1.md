# Returns and Refund Policy

## Return window

Customers may return unused products within **14 days** of purchase, with proof of purchase.

## Refund timing

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 business days.

## Condition of returned goods

Products must be unused and in their original packaging. Damaged-in-transit claims must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.

## Exceptions

Store managers may approve a return outside the window as a goodwill gesture. The reason must be recorded on the return.
