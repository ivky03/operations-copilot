# Returns and Refund Policy

This version replaces the policy dated 1 January 2025. Purchases made before 1 March 2026 follow the previous policy.

## Return window

Customers may return unused products within **30 days** of purchase, with proof of purchase. The window was extended from 14 days to 30 days on 1 March 2026.

## Refund timing

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Condition of returned goods

Products must be unused and in their original packaging. Products damaged in transit may be returned at any point within the 30-day window with a photo of the damage.

## Partial returns

Customers may return some units of a line and keep the rest. The refund is the returned quantity multiplied by the price actually paid, including any promotion discount.

## Exceptions

Store managers may approve a return outside the window as a goodwill gesture. The reason must be recorded on the return.

## Reporting treatment

Refunds are reported as refund value in the month the refund was processed and are attributed to the store that made the original sale.
