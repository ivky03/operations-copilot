# Month-End Close Procedure

**Finance only.**

## Timeline

- Business day 1: sales, cancellation and refund data for the closed month is frozen.
- Business day 2: net sales by country is reconciled to payment-provider settlements.
- Business day 3: the management pack is issued to the leadership team.

## Late adjustments

Cancellations or refunds processed after the close are reported in the month they happen, never restated into the closed month. This matches the Sales Metric Definitions.

## Sign-off

The Finance Controller signs off the close. Variances above 1% between net sales and settlements need a written explanation.
