# Shipping and Delivery Standards

## Delivery times

| Destination | Standard delivery |
|---|---|
| United Kingdom, Ireland | 2–4 business days |
| Germany, Netherlands | 3–5 business days |
| France, Spain | 4–6 business days |

Furniture is delivered by a two-person team in 5–10 business days in all countries.

## Delivery charges

Delivery is free on web orders of €75 or more. Below that, a flat €5.95 charge applies. Furniture delivery is always €39.

## Dispatch cut-off

Web orders placed before 14:00 local time on a business day are dispatched the same day. Once dispatched, an order can no longer be cancelled.
