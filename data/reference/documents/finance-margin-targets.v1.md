# Gross Margin Targets 2026

**Confidential: Finance only.**

## Targets by category

| Category | Target gross margin |
|---|---|
| Lighting | 55% |
| Kitchen | 48% |
| Textiles | 60% |
| Furniture | 42% |
| Decor | 58% |

## Promotions

Promotions must not take a category's monthly margin more than 5 percentage points below target. SPRING10 was approved on this basis.

## Actual margins

Actual margins are calculated in the finance system from product costs, which are not held in the operational sales tables.
