# Sales Metric Definitions

These definitions are the single source of truth for sales reporting at Brindlewood Home. All amounts are in EUR, the reporting currency for every country. Reporting periods are calendar months in local store time.

## Gross sales

Gross sales is the value of all sales lines: quantity multiplied by the unit price actually charged, after any promotion. Gross sales does not deduct cancellations or refunds.

## Cancellation value

Cancellation value is the value of cancelled order lines, expressed as a positive amount. A cancellation is counted in the month in which the cancellation happened, not the month of the original order. An order placed on 28 February and cancelled on 1 March therefore adds to February gross sales and to March cancellation value.

## Refund value

Refund value is the amount refunded to customers for returned products, counted in the month the refund was processed.

## Net sales

Net sales = gross sales − cancellation value − refund value.

Each component is attributed to the period in which it happened. Net sales for a month can therefore differ from "orders placed that month that were kept".

## Orders and average order value

An order is one sales invoice. The order count includes orders that were later cancelled. Average order value (AOV) is gross sales divided by the number of orders.

## Cancellation rate

Cancellation rate is the number of cancelled orders divided by the number of orders, for the same period and scope.

## What these metrics do not include

Sales metrics do not include product cost, so they cannot be used to calculate profit or margin. Margin reporting is owned by Finance.
