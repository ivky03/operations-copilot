# Order Cancellation Policy

## When customers can cancel

Customers may cancel an order free of charge at any time before it is dispatched. In stores, a purchase can be cancelled at the till on the same day if the customer has not left with the goods. Once an order has been dispatched, it can no longer be cancelled and must follow the returns process instead.

## How cancellations are recorded

A cancellation is recorded as a separate cancellation invoice. Its number is the original invoice number prefixed with "C" (for example, INV-100031 becomes CINV-100031). Each cancelled line appears with a negative quantity and the original unit price. Cancellations are always for the whole order; partial cancellations are not supported.

## Refunds for cancelled orders

Cancelled orders are refunded in full to the original payment method within three business days. Cancellation refunds are not recorded as returns.

## Reporting treatment

Cancellations are reported as cancellation value in the month the cancellation happened and are deducted from gross sales when calculating net sales. See Sales Metric Definitions.

## Cancelled orders and returns

A cancelled order cannot also be returned, because the goods never reached the customer.
