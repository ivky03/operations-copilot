# Pricing and Promotions Policy

## List prices

Every product has a single list price in EUR, used in all countries and channels. List prices are reviewed quarterly by the Commercial team.

## Recording prices on sales

The unit price on a sales line is the price actually charged, after any promotion. The list price in the product catalog may therefore differ from the price on historical sales lines.

## Promotions

Promotions are approved by the Commercial team and identified by a promotion code recorded on each discounted line. Promotions cannot be combined.

## Current and recent promotions

**SPRING10:** 10% off all Textiles products from 1 April to 30 April 2026, in all stores and web shops. The discounted price is recorded as the unit price, and the code SPRING10 is recorded on the line.

## Price matching

Brindlewood Home does not price-match competitors.
