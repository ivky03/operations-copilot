# Stock Count and Inventory Reporting

## What the daily stock figure means
Stock is recorded **at the end of each day**, for every product in every store and web shop. Each figure is a balance at one moment, not a flow. To report stock "on 31 March", use that day's figure only. **Never add up stock across several days**: the same units would be counted again every day.

## Stockouts
A product is **out of stock** in a store when its end-of-day stock is zero. Stockout days are counted per store and product: one product out of stock in two stores for three days is six stockout days.

## Movements
Stock goes down when items are sold and up when a purchase order is received. Cancelled customer orders and returned items go back into stock on the day of the cancellation or return. Stock never goes below zero in reporting; a sale made while the system showed zero is corrected at the next count.

## New stores
A store has stock records only from its opening date. FR Online, opened on 2 March 2026, has no stock records before that date.
