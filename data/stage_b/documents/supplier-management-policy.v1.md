# Supplier Management Policy

## Preferred suppliers
Each product category has one **preferred supplier**, which receives all automatic purchase orders, and one alternative supplier used only when the preferred supplier cannot deliver.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are 30 days. Terms of 45 or 60 days are agreed only with suppliers outside our home markets or with long production times.

## Performance review
Supply Chain reviews each preferred supplier every quarter on three measures: on-time delivery (share of received purchase orders that arrived by the agreed date), completeness (units received versus units ordered) and cancellations. A preferred supplier whose late rate exceeds **20%** for two quarters in a row is put on an improvement plan.

## Supplier code of conduct
All suppliers must sign the supplier code of conduct before their first order. Purchase prices and supplier contracts are confidential and are never shared with customers.
