# Budget Guidelines 2026

## Budget lines
Each department has a monthly cost budget split by cost category. Budgets are fixed in December for the following year and are not changed during the year.

## Actuals
Actual costs are recorded only after a month has been **closed** (see the Month-End Close Procedure). Until then the actual is empty, not zero. As of the end of April 2026, April has not been closed.

## Variance
Variance is actual cost minus budgeted cost: a positive variance is an overspend. Compare only closed months; including an open month makes spending look lower than it is. Department heads explain any variance above 10% of budget at the monthly review.

## Sales targets
Monthly net sales targets per store are set by Finance from the previous trading pattern and the store's growth plan. Targets are confidential to Finance and Store Managers.
