# Staffing and Scheduling Policy

## Who counts in headcount
**Headcount** is the number of people employed on a given date. Leavers count until their last day (end date) and joiners from their first day. Unless a date is given, Brindlewood Home reports headcount on the last day of the reporting period.

## Roles
Each physical store has one Store Manager and three to five Sales Associates. Each web shop has Fulfilment Associates who pick and pack its orders. Customer Service Agents work centrally and are not attached to any store.

## Contracts and shifts
Full-time contracts are 37.5 hours a week over five shifts. Part-time contracts are 16, 20 or 24 hours a week over two or three shifts. Rotas are published two weeks in advance. Overtime of up to one hour per shift may be worked with the Store Manager's approval.

## Hiring for launches
New stores and web shops are staffed three weeks before opening. Customer service gained one agent on 2 March 2026 for the French launch.
