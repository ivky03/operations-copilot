# Purchasing and Receiving Procedure

## Automatic reordering
Every store and web shop holds its own stock. When a product's end-of-day stock falls to **2 units or fewer**, a purchase order is raised automatically the same day with the category's **preferred supplier**. Only one purchase order per product and store can be open at a time.

Standard order quantities are **12 units**, or **4 units for furniture**. Discontinued products are never reordered.

## Lead times and lateness
Each supplier has an agreed lead time in days. The expected delivery date is the order date plus that lead time. A delivery is **late** if it arrives after the expected date; early deliveries are accepted. Supplier lateness is reviewed quarterly using the share of received purchase orders that arrived late.

## Receiving
Goods are counted on arrival. If fewer units arrive than were ordered, the purchase order is closed as **partially received**; the shortfall is not re-ordered automatically. A supplier may cancel a purchase order it cannot fulfil; nothing is received and the store reorders when stock is next checked.

## Purchase orders are not customer orders
"Orders" in sales reporting always means customer orders. Purchase orders are what we buy from suppliers, valued at **purchase cost**, which is not the selling price.
