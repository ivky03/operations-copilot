# Absence Policy

## Recording absences
An absence is a scheduled shift that was not worked. Every absence is recorded with a reason: **sick**, **holiday** (approved leave taken on a scheduled day) or **no_show** (absent without notice).

## Absence rate
The absence rate is the share of scheduled shifts not worked, for any reason. People Operations also tracks the **unplanned** absence rate, which counts only sick and no-show shifts.

## Reporting sickness
Employees must tell their manager before the start of the shift. From the fourth consecutive sick day, a doctor's note is required.

## Follow-up
Two no-shows within three months lead to a meeting with the Store Manager. Managers review absence monthly; a store with an absence rate above **6%** in a month reviews its rota with People Operations.
