# Store Dashboards vs Reported Sales

## Two different numbers
Store dashboards show **dashboard revenue**: the value of orders placed each day, refreshed overnight. It is useful for watching daily trading, but it is **not** the company's reported sales figure.

The reported figure is **net sales**: gross sales minus cancellations minus refunds, each counted in the month it happened (see the Sales Metric Definitions).

## Why they differ
- Dashboard revenue never subtracts cancellations, even when the order is cancelled later.
- Dashboard revenue never subtracts refunds.
- Dashboard revenue is fixed on the day the order was placed; net sales moves cancellations and refunds into the month they happened.

## Which to use
Always use **net sales** in management reports, targets and any figure shared outside the store. Dashboard revenue is for in-day operational monitoring only.
