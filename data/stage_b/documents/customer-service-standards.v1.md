# Customer Service Standards

## Response time targets
First response to the customer, measured from when the ticket is opened:

| Channel | Target |
|---|---|
| Phone | answered within 5 minutes |
| Chat | within 15 minutes |
| Email | within 24 hours |

## Priorities
Delivery and billing problems are **high** priority. Product questions and store queries are normal or low priority.

## Customer satisfaction (CSAT)
After a ticket is closed, the customer is asked to rate the service from **1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent)**. CSAT is the **average rating over the customers who answered**; unanswered surveys are not counted as low scores. About half of customers answer. The target is an average of **4.0 or higher**.

## Escalation
A ticket open for more than three days is reviewed by a team lead. Complaints that cannot be resolved in store are handed to Customer Operations.
