# Delivery Performance Measurement

## Promised delivery date
When an online order is dispatched, the customer is promised delivery within a number of calendar days from dispatch:

| Destination | Promise |
|---|---|
| United Kingdom, Ireland | 4 days |
| Germany, Netherlands | 5 days |
| France, Spain | 6 days |
| Any order containing furniture | 10 days |

Orders placed before 14:00 are dispatched the same day; later orders the next day.

## On-time delivery rate
A shipment is **on time** if it is delivered on or before the promised date. The **on-time delivery rate** is on-time shipments divided by **delivered** shipments; parcels still in transit are left out until they arrive. The target is **90%**.

## Scope
Only online orders are shipped. Purchases in physical stores are taken home by the customer, and cancelled orders are never dispatched.
