# Carrier Agreement: Rhein Logistik

## Service

Rhein Logistik delivers parcels in Germany and the Netherlands.

## Price and speed

Contract price EUR 4.40 per parcel. Contractual delivery within 5 calendar days of dispatch.

## Damage and loss

Insurance covers up to EUR 500 per parcel; claims within 10 days.
