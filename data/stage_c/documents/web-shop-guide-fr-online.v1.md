# Web Shop Guide: FR Online

## About this web shop

FR Online serves customers in France. It opened on 2026-03-02 and is part of the Southwest Europe region.

## Orders and dispatch

Orders placed before 14:00 are dispatched the same working day from the regional distribution centre. Furniture always ships by two-person delivery.

## Customer contact

Customer questions for FR Online go to the central customer service team by email, chat or phone; the web shop has no local staff.
