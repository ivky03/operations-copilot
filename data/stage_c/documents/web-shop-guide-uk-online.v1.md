# Web Shop Guide: UK Online

## About this web shop

UK Online serves customers in United Kingdom. It opened on 2018-01-15 and is part of the UK & Ireland region.

## Orders and dispatch

Orders placed before 14:00 are dispatched the same working day from the regional distribution centre. Furniture always ships by two-person delivery.

## Customer contact

Customer questions for UK Online go to the central customer service team by email, chat or phone; the web shop has no local staff.
