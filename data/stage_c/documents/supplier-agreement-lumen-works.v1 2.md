# Supplier Agreement: Lumen Works

## Supplier

Lumen Works (Poland) supplies Lighting and is a preferred supplier.

## Ordering

The minimum order value is EUR 800. Standard lead time is 12 days from order to delivery at the distribution centre.

## Payment

Invoices are payable 30 days from the invoice date.

## Quality

Up to 1.5% of units per delivery may be defective without penalty; above that, the supplier credits the full value of defective units.

## Rebates

Commercial terms: 2% rebate on annual purchases above EUR 20,000.
