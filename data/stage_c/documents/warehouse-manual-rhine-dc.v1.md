# Warehouse Manual: Rhine DC

## Site

Rhine DC (WH2) in Duisburg is run by Kontor 3PL and supplies the Central Europe region. It has 5,600 pallet spaces.

## Receiving

Supplier deliveries are received 07:00-15:00, Monday to Friday. Deliveries outside the window are refused unless booked in advance.

## Stock counts

Stock is counted every Monday; the count is the figure reported for the week.
