# Carrier Agreement: TwoMen Freight

## Service

TwoMen Freight delivers parcels in all countries, for furniture.

## Price and speed

Contract price EUR 39.00 per parcel. Contractual delivery within 10 calendar days of dispatch.

## Damage and loss

Insurance covers up to EUR 2,500 per parcel; claims within 7 days, with photos.
