# Supplier Agreement: Frame Republic

## Supplier

Frame Republic (Netherlands) supplies Decor and is a secondary supplier.

## Ordering

The minimum order value is EUR 400. Standard lead time is 8 days from order to delivery at the distribution centre.

## Payment

Invoices are payable 30 days from the invoice date.

## Quality

Up to 1.5% of units per delivery may be defective without penalty; above that, the supplier credits the full value of defective units.

## Rebates

Commercial terms: no rebate.
