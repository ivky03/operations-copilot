# Web Shop Guide: DE Online

## About this web shop

DE Online serves customers in Germany. It opened on 2019-01-10 and is part of the Central Europe region.

## Orders and dispatch

Orders placed before 14:00 are dispatched the same working day from the regional distribution centre. Furniture always ships by two-person delivery.

## Customer contact

Customer questions for DE Online go to the central customer service team by email, chat or phone; the web shop has no local staff.
