# Supplier Agreement: Ferro Kitchenware

## Supplier

Ferro Kitchenware (Portugal) supplies Kitchen and is a preferred supplier.

## Ordering

The minimum order value is EUR 600. Standard lead time is 10 days from order to delivery at the distribution centre.

## Payment

Invoices are payable 30 days from the invoice date.

## Quality

Up to 2.0% of units per delivery may be defective without penalty; above that, the supplier credits the full value of defective units.

## Rebates

Commercial terms: 1.5% rebate on annual purchases above EUR 15,000.
