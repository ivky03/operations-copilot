# Campaign Brief: Winter lighting

## Campaign

Winter lighting (CMP-02) runs on social from 2026-01-10 to 2026-02-15 with a media budget of EUR 6,500.

## Audience

Target audience: people interested in home decor, aged 25-45.

## Success measure

The campaign is judged on reach and video views. Attributed revenue from the ad platform is reported for information only; it is not net sales.
