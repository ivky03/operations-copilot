# Supplier Agreement: Porto Linen Co.

## Supplier

Porto Linen Co. (Portugal) supplies Textiles and is a preferred supplier.

## Ordering

The minimum order value is EUR 500. Standard lead time is 9 days from order to delivery at the distribution centre.

## Payment

Invoices are payable 30 days from the invoice date.

## Quality

Up to 1.0% of units per delivery may be defective without penalty; above that, the supplier credits the full value of defective units.

## Rebates

Commercial terms: 3% rebate on annual purchases above EUR 25,000.
