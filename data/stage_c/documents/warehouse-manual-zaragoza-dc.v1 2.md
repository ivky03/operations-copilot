# Warehouse Manual: Zaragoza DC

## Site

Zaragoza DC (WH3) in Zaragoza is run by Iberia Fulfilment and supplies the Southwest Europe region. It has 3,100 pallet spaces.

## Receiving

Supplier deliveries are received 08:00-16:00, Monday to Friday. Deliveries outside the window are refused unless booked in advance.

## Stock counts

Stock is counted every Monday; the count is the figure reported for the week.
