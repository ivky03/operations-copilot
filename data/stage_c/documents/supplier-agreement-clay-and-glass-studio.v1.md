# Supplier Agreement: Clay & Glass Studio

## Supplier

Clay & Glass Studio (Spain) supplies Decor and is a preferred supplier.

## Ordering

The minimum order value is EUR 700. Standard lead time is 11 days from order to delivery at the distribution centre.

## Payment

Invoices are payable 30 days from the invoice date.

## Quality

Up to 2.0% of units per delivery may be defective without penalty; above that, the supplier credits the full value of defective units.

## Rebates

Commercial terms: 2% rebate on annual purchases above EUR 12,000.
