# Store Guide: London Flagship

## About the store

London Flagship in United Kingdom opened on 2019-04-01. The store manager is Ana B..

## Opening hours

Monday to Saturday 09:00-20:00 and Sunday 11:00-17:00 unless noted. The store opens until 21:00 on Thursdays for late shopping.

## Parking and access

Customer parking is at the Kingsway car park; validate tickets at the till for two free hours.

## Deliveries

Deliveries arrive at the Drury Lane loading bay between 06:00 and 08:00.
