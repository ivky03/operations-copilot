# Supplier Agreement: Baltic Timber Design

## Supplier

Baltic Timber Design (Lithuania) supplies Furniture and is a secondary supplier.

## Ordering

The minimum order value is EUR 3,000. Standard lead time is 28 days from order to delivery at the distribution centre.

## Payment

Invoices are payable 60 days from the invoice date.

## Quality

Up to 1.0% of units per delivery may be defective without penalty; above that, the supplier credits the full value of defective units.

## Rebates

Commercial terms: no rebate.
