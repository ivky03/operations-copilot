# Store Guide: Madrid Store

## About the store

Madrid Store in Spain opened on 2023-05-01. The store manager is Xenia B..

## Opening hours

Monday to Saturday 09:00-20:00 and Sunday 11:00-17:00 unless noted. Opening hours follow the handbook; the store closes at 15:00 on 6 January.

## Parking and access

Customer parking is shared with the Gran Vía shopping centre, free for two hours.

## Deliveries

Deliveries arrive at the shopping centre's service yard, dock 4, before 08:00.
