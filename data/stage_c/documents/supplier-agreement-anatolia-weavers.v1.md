# Supplier Agreement: Anatolia Weavers

## Supplier

Anatolia Weavers (Turkey) supplies Textiles and is a secondary supplier.

## Ordering

The minimum order value is EUR 2,000. Standard lead time is 21 days from order to delivery at the distribution centre.

## Payment

Invoices are payable 60 days from the invoice date.

## Quality

Up to 3.0% of units per delivery may be defective without penalty; above that, the supplier credits the full value of defective units.

## Rebates

Commercial terms: no rebate.
