# Store Guide: Amsterdam Store

## About the store

Amsterdam Store in Netherlands opened on 2022-03-01. The store manager is Tara C..

## Opening hours

Monday to Saturday 09:00-20:00 and Sunday 11:00-17:00 unless noted. Opening hours follow the handbook with no local changes.

## Parking and access

Bicycle parking for 40 bikes is in front of the store; there is no car park.

## Deliveries

Deliveries come by small electric van to the canal-side door before 09:00.
