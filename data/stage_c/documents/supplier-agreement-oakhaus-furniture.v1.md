# Supplier Agreement: Oakhaus Furniture

## Supplier

Oakhaus Furniture (Germany) supplies Furniture and is a preferred supplier.

## Ordering

The minimum order value is EUR 2,500. Standard lead time is 20 days from order to delivery at the distribution centre.

## Payment

Invoices are payable 45 days from the invoice date.

## Quality

Up to 0.5% of units per delivery may be defective without penalty; above that, the supplier credits the full value of defective units.

## Rebates

Commercial terms: 2.5% rebate on annual purchases above EUR 40,000.
