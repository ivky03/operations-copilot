# Brindlewood Rewards Terms

## Earning

Members earn one point per euro spent, rounded down, on every order line that is not cancelled.

## Redeeming

500 points can be exchanged for a EUR 10 voucher.

## Tiers

Silver from 600 points and Gold from 1,500 points held.

## Expiry

Points expire 12 months after they are earned.
