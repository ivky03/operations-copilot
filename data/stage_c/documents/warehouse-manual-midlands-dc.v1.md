# Warehouse Manual: Midlands DC

## Site

Midlands DC (WH1) in Coventry is run by Northline Logistics and supplies the UK & Ireland region. It has 4,200 pallet spaces.

## Receiving

Supplier deliveries are received 06:00-14:00, Monday to Friday. Deliveries outside the window are refused unless booked in advance.

## Stock counts

Stock is counted every Monday; the count is the figure reported for the week.
